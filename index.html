How the matchmaker ranks borrowers

Every open listing gets a match score from 0 to 100 points for you specifically. Scores are computed by the Java matchmaker backend each time you move a control.

Net expected ROI

Net ROI = interest rate − default probability × 75% × 100

The return you can expect after the average loss from defaults, assuming 75% of a defaulted loan is lost.

Risk fit · up to 40 pts

risk index = default prob × 50
risk fit = 40 − 5 × |appetite − risk index|

Borrowers whose risk matches your appetite score highest. At appetite 1 the ideal is about 2% default risk; at 5 it is 10%.

Return · up to 35 pts

return = 2 × net ROI × (0.6 + 0.4 × appetite ÷ 10)

Higher net ROI scores more, and counts for more as your appetite rises.

Capital fit · up to 25 pts

capital fit = 25 × (1 − |requested − capital| ÷ max(requested, capital))

Requests close in size to your available capital score highest.

Rationale

Each recommendation carries a one-line rationale from the backend: conservative investors (1–3) see solvency-based reasons, aggressive investors (8–10) see yield-based reasons, and balanced investors see a blend.

Allocation

Your capital is split across the top 5 in proportion to match score, never more than what a borrower requested.

Verified borrowers only

When the filter is on, the backend leaves out any borrower who has not verified both Aadhaar and PAN before ranking.
