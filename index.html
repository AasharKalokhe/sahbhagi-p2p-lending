How the matchmaker ranks borrowers

Every open listing gets a fit score from 0 to 100 for you specifically. It combines four factors, and the weights move with your risk appetite.

1. Default risk

A logistic scorecard estimates the probability of default (PD) from credit score, debt-to-income ratio, on-time repayment history, work/business vintage, tenure and KYC status.

2. Expected ROI

ROI p.a. = interest rate − 1% fee − (PD × 50% loss ÷ avg. loan life)

Higher-rate loans only rank well if their extra interest more than covers their expected losses.

3. Capacity

Your amount is split across 5 borrowers. Listings that can absorb your full share score higher; nearly-funded loans score lower as your amount grows.

4. Trust

Aadhaar + PAN verification and repayment track record. Unverified borrowers also carry a default-risk premium.

The weights

On top of this, any borrower whose default risk exceeds your tolerance (3% at appetite 1, up to 23% at appetite 10) gets a penalty that grows with the gap. That is why a conservative investor never sees E-grade loans at the top, while an aggressive investor gets higher-yield loans that are still priced well.

Allocation

Your amount is split across the top 5 in proportion to fit score, capped at 35% per borrower and at what each borrower still needs. Anything that can't be placed is shown as unallocated.
